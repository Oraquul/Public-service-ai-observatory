# Join the Think Tank

Fellow AIG Public Leaders Programme alumni are invited to opt in, in their own names, as contributors, reviewers or case partners. Public servants, academics, AI practitioners and colleagues in governance, privacy and service design are invited on the same terms. You do not need to write a full article.

No alumnus is named on this page. Participation is voluntary. There is no membership fee in the founding pilot. Membership does not guarantee publication, paid work or research authorship. It does not imply endorsement by the Aig-Imoukhuede Foundation, the University of Oxford, the Nigerian Export Promotion Council, or any other institution.

## Ways to contribute

- A field note of about 300 to 500 words on a service problem, a lesson or a question worth investigating.
- A nomination of one public or authorised case, or a structured interview using public or properly authorised material.
- A review of subject accuracy, research quality, privacy, governance or practical feasibility.
- Help testing a replication pack, only where the institution has given authority and the evaluation is defined.
- A low-effort alternative: a 20-minute interview, a case nomination with sources, or a 30-minute review. An editor can help turn that into an accurate publication. You approve the final attributed version.

Do not send confidential material. Public sources and authorised, sanitised material are the only basis for a published case.

## Roles and time

These are the roles in the operating playbook. Only the founder and editor role is filled. The others are open. The hours are initial assumptions, not a contract.

| Role | Accountable work | Initial time assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder and editor | Editorial direction and final publication decisions. Held by Arc Benedict Itegbe. | 4 to 5 hours a week |
| Managing editor and researcher | Intake, source checks, drafts, the calendar and contributor support. Open. | 10 to 12 hours a week |
| Producer and automation operator | Video editing, drafts in the publishing flow, backups and basic analytics. Open. | 6 to 8 hours a week |
| Rotating subject reviewer | Domain claims and implementation. Open. | 1 to 2 hours for an assigned case |
| Rotating governance reviewer | Privacy, permissions, harm and disclosure. Open. | About 1 hour for an assigned case |
| Founding circle | Priorities, independence, appeals and continuity. Proposed, not yet named. | About 1 hour a month |

The author of a piece cannot be its only reviewer. A material conflict means recusal. If only the editor is available, the journal will publish less rather than skip review.

## Rhythm

The proposed rhythm is a monthly 60-minute case clinic, a quarterly editorial council and a quarterly session for newcomers. A clinic would give about 10 minutes to the problem, 20 to the case, 20 to adaptation and 10 to agreed actions. No clinic is scheduled on this site yet. A sanitised summary would be circulated. Recording would require consent.

## Conduct

Be respectful and non-partisan. Do not disclose confidential information. Declare financial interests. Distinguish personal views from institutional statements. Share sources and corrections. Do not disguise a sales pitch as a case. No one should be coerced by a workplace into contributing. Repeated harassment or misrepresentation can lead to removal through a documented process. You may withdraw a contribution before publication on the terms agreed at submission.

A contribution to the Think Tank is not consent to doctoral research. Ethics approval and a separate consent would be required before any note was used as research data.

## After you send the form

You should receive an acknowledgement. The form service may ask you to confirm your email. Receipt is not approval. If the contribution fits, a first small task is the aim within 14 days. The editor will say what should not be submitted. Unsuitable material will not be forced into print.
