# Privacy

This notice explains the forms on this static journal. It is not a data-protection assessment of a government service, and it is not legal advice.

## Who is responsible

Arc Benedict Itegbe, founder and editor, is the person responsible for material sent through the forms. This is a personal initiative. It is not a claim that the Aig-Imoukhuede Foundation, the University of Oxford or the Nigerian Export Promotion Council processes these messages.

## What the forms collect

The join form asks for your name, email, area of expertise, preferred contribution and availability. Institution, a note, and any interest you choose to declare are optional. A required checkbox records that you are opting in to be contacted. A second checkbox, off unless you turn it on, is the separate request for The Public Service AI Brief.

The contact form asks for your name, email, a topic and a message.

The newsletter form asks for an email, an optional name, and a required checkbox that you want the briefing.

Do not include staff identifiers, personnel files, confidential institutional records, or personal data about service users. If a message contains that material, it should be deleted rather than used.

## Who receives a message

The forms post to Formspree at `https://formspree.io/f/mvkzbdqq`. Formspree processes the submission so it can be delivered to the editor. Formspree’s own privacy information is on [formspree.io](https://formspree.io/). The page address and the time of submission may be included with the delivery. This site itself does not run an account system and does not set its own cookies.

If you use the Watch page and choose to load the YouTube playlist, your browser then connects to YouTube, which may store cookies. The playlist is not loaded until you use that control. A plain link to YouTube is also provided.

## Newsletter and membership are separate

Asking for the briefing does not join the Think Tank. Joining the Think Tank does not, by itself, add you to the briefing. You can request either, neither, or both. You can ask to withdraw either request through the [contact form](/contact/).

## Retention, as a starting point only

The playbook suggests operational starting points, not statutory retention periods: unaccepted applications discarded after 90 days unless the person asks to remain in a pool; active membership reviewed annually; raw interview recordings deleted 12 months after publication unless a separate archival or research agreement says otherwise. Those periods have not been adopted as a finished schedule. Until that review is done, messages will be kept only long enough to reply, to log a correction or a privacy request, and to avoid losing the record of what was asked. They will not be sold and will not be used as a marketing list for a third party.

## Your requests

You may ask for access to the message you sent, ask for a correction, or ask for the request to be withdrawn. Use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”. Withdrawal stops further use of the contact for the purpose you name. It may not erase a copy already required for a correction log or a security record, and that limit will be explained if it applies.

## Research

A form submission is not consent to doctoral research. The editor will not treat a join note, a contact message or a newsletter request as research data. A study would need its own ethics approval and a separate consent. See the [DBA Research Diary](/diary/).
