## AI in Public Service

The video series *AI in Public Service with Benedict Itegbe* is coming soon. No episode has been recorded for this journal, and none is embedded here.

When it starts, each episode is meant to explain a service problem, the evidence, the costs, the safeguards and the adaptation steps. Proposed ideas will be labelled as proposals. Measured results will carry their evidence and their limits. The series description prepared for that launch is: practical uses of AI and automation in governance and economic development, with the case article and the sources beside the film.

Planned formats, not a catalogue of finished films:

- Explainers of about eight minutes.
- Interviews of 20 to 30 minutes.
- Short clips of 35 to 60 seconds, each making one point.
- Clinics of about 60 minutes.

The first rehearsal script, which has not been filmed, sits on the [export-enquiry case](/cases/case-ng-001/#rehearsal-script) and keeps the E0 disclosure in the narration. A recording would not be published until its claims match the corrected article.

Synthetic demonstration footage and AI-assisted production, if they are ever used, will be disclosed. The journal will not use a generic robot image as evidence of a deployment.
