## The synthetic case

[An export-enquiry assistant that knows when to stop](/cases/case-ng-001/) is a simulated case built on synthetic data. The NEPC-themed scenario is fictional. The public e-registration page is cited as background for registration information, checked on 2 October 2026. No deployment, backlog, saving or performance result is attributed to the Nigerian Export Promotion Council.

The minutes, correct-answer counts and naira totals in that case are constructed fixtures. No language model was called to produce them. Fifteen local software checks were executed in the rehearsal. They do not show that a production system is safe. Draft v1, which misstated the cost comparison, was not published here.
