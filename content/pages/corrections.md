# Corrections

A reader who finds a wrong name, figure, source or evidence label can ask for a correction. The editor logs the request, the affected claim and the pages or other assets it touches.

## How to ask

Use the [contact form](/contact/) and choose “Correction”. Include the page address, the sentence you believe is wrong, and the source that supports the correction. If you do not want to use the form, send the same details through the person who shared the founding note with you and ask them to pass it to Arc Benedict Itegbe.

## What happens next

The working standard is to respond to a verified report with a public correction within two working days where the fault is clear. That interval is a target, not a guarantee that a roster is staffed overnight. A material change creates a new version. Dependent video notes, templates and briefing text are updated or marked stale together. The corrected passage stays visible enough that a reader can see what changed.

An editorial correction is not the same as an incident inside a public service. If a real service had released an unsafe answer, the service owner would use that institution’s own process. Nothing on this site is a live service reply.
