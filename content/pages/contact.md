# Contact

Write to Arc Benedict Itegbe, founder and editor, about a correction, a privacy request, an accessibility barrier, or a general question. This form does not join you to the Think Tank and does not subscribe you to the briefing. Those choices are separate, on [Join the Think Tank](/join/) and on the homepage.

A direct email address is not published on this site. If you prefer not to use the form, reply through the person who shared the founding note with you and ask them to pass your message on.
